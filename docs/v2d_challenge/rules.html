Teams & participation

Submit one registration form per team and list all team members and their affiliations. A team may enter any subset of the three challenge tracks. Organizer entries are non-competing.

Awaiting confirmation

Team-size limit, participant eligibility, and rules for changing team membership or merging teams.

Intellectual property & licenses

The current award requirements include an agreed code release under a research license and a short technical report.

Awaiting confirmation

  • Ownership of submissions and any rights granted to the organizers.

Leaderboard & evaluation

Track metrics are described in the challenge tracks. The challenge uses held-out ground truth; selected top solutions also receive broader evaluation after the challenge.

Proposed public/private evaluation policy

Public leaderboard scores would use only a subset of the evaluation data and remain provisional. A separate private holdout would determine final rankings; its scores and rankings would remain hidden during the challenge.

This proposed final-ranking evaluation is separate from the broader post-challenge evaluation of selected top solutions.

Awaiting confirmation

The public/private split, final scoring procedure, tie-breaks, which final submission is evaluated, and the timing of final scoring and ranking verification relative to the winner announcement. These details must be published with the final rules and reflected in the evaluation service.

Awards & winner materials

Current eligibility requirements are a valid final submission, agreed code release under a research license, and the short technical report. Organizer entries do not compete for awards.

CoRL presentation

Top teams are invited to present at the CoRL 2026 tutorial, with remote participation available. Whether a presentation is required to claim a prize is awaiting confirmation.

Materials & award verification

The final rules will specify the required winner materials, delivery instructions, evaluation period, and exact submission deadline.

Proposed late-materials policy: a team that misses the confirmed materials deadline would lose award eligibility, and the next eligible team would be considered after verification.

Awaiting confirmation

The presentation requirement, materials and report deadlines, extension policy, and award-reassignment procedure. No new deadline or forfeiture condition is established by this draft.
