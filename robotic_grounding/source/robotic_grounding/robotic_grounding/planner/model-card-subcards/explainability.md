Intended Task/Domain:                                                                                  |  Robotics
Model Type:                                                                                            |  Autoregressive Transformer (MotionBricks root + pose modules)
Intended Users:                                                                                        |  Robotics researchers and engineers building whole-body humanoid manipulation and reference-tracking policies. The model converts end-effector targets into dense whole-body reference motion for synthetic-data generation and reinforcement-learning pipelines.
Output:                                                                                                |  Other (specify): Full-body humanoid robot joint trajectory
Describe how the model works:                                                                          |  Desired end-effector (wrist) and root pose targets are heading-canonicalized and tokenized (4 frames per token). A root transformer module predicts the root trajectory and a pose transformer module predicts per-body transforms conditioned on the end-effector targets; the bundled humanoid kinematics recovers joint angles. Long sequences are produced by chunked autoregressive inference with overlap blending.
Name the adversely impacted groups this has been tested to deliver comparable outcomes regardless of:  |  Not Applicable
Technical Limitations & Mitigation:                                                                    |  Generated motion may exhibit artifacts such as foot skating or chunk-boundary discontinuities; the planner applies overlap blending and optional boundary smoothing to mitigate these. End-effector tracking accuracy degrades for targets far outside the training distribution or kinematically infeasible for the fixed G1 skeleton. The model outputs motion for a single fixed humanoid skeleton (29 G1 joints), is not aware of scene objects or collisions, and produces kinematic (not dynamics-validated) references — physical feasibility is enforced downstream by the SONIC controller and simulation.
Verified to have met prescribed NVIDIA quality standards:                                              |  Not Applicable
Performance Metrics:                                                                                   |  End-effector (wrist) tracking accuracy (position error in mm, orientation error in degrees), whole-body joint accuracy (MPJPE), and motion smoothness / foot-skating.
Potential Known Risks:                                                                                  |  Generated motions may be physically implausible or fail to track the requested end-effector targets, particularly for out-of-distribution inputs.
