Output:                                                                                                |  Other (specify): Quantized latent tokens and whole-body robot joint action commands
Describe how the model works:                                                                          |  A single unified policy trained with reinforcement learning to track human motion. The encoder maps the current observation (robot state + motion-tracking reference / commands) into a quantized latent token; the decoder maps that token plus an observation history into whole-body joint action commands.

Technical Limitations & Mitigation:                                                                    |  Tracking quality depends on the quality and feasibility of the reference motion. Low-latency variants use a reduced-lookahead design that can reduce tracking fidelity. Real-time performance is contingent on hardware. The policy controls the 29 G1 body joints only; hand/finger control is handled separately.
